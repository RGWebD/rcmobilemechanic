FAQ

Quick answers.

Still have a question? Call or text (904) 750-1894.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. RC Mobile Mechanic is a mobile service. Customers have been helped at their homes, at their front door, at their workplace, at their apartment and in parking lots, so you don't need a tow or a ride to a shop.

What are your hours?

RC Mobile Mechanic is listed as open 24 hours. Call or text (904) 750-1894 any time, day or night, and we'll let you know when we can get to you.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Jacksonville, Orange Park and the surrounding Northeast Florida area. Not sure we reach you? Call or text your location and we'll confirm.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit cards, debit cards and NFC mobile payments (tap-to-pay phone wallets).

Is language assistance available?

Yes. Language assistance is offered. Just let us know when you call or text.

What vehicles do you work on?

Cars, SUVs and trucks. Customers have had work done on Toyota, Honda, BMW, Mercedes, Jeep, Ram, Chevy and Hyundai vehicles. Call with your year, make and model to confirm.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text (904) 750-1894 with your vehicle's year, make and model, what's going on, and where it's parked. You can also fill out the request form on this page to build that text for you.
